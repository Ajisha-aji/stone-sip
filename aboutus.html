ABOUT US

Our journey began with a single cup of coffee.

Stone Sip was created with one simple goal: to make cold brew that tastes great and fits effortlessly into everyday life. We believe good coffee shouldn't be complicated. Just carefully brewed, thoughtfully sourced, and ready when you are.

We brew slowly, never rush the process, and use carefully selected beans to create cold brew that's smooth, balanced, and easy to enjoy anytime, anywhere.
